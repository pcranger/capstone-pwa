# Eye Navi evidence to CrossWise screen mapping

13 October 2026. All 40 historical atlas records are preserved below with uncropped reference frames and original observations. These are evidence, not application assets to use as button backgrounds. Maps/identities intentionally obscured in the source remain obscured. Later capabilities without a filmed layout are marked published/proposed in EYENAVI_SCREEN_SPEC.md.

See [implementation plan](EYENAVI_PARITY_PLAN.md), [screen specification](EYENAVI_SCREEN_SPEC.md) and [voice specification](EYENAVI_VOICE_SPEC.md).

## S01 — Historical launch screen

**CrossWise pages:** CW01; **source:** [video at 8 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QffWGq3ocQs&t=8s).

![S01 full uncropped reference frame](../../capstone_research/EyeNavi_Research/evidence/QffWGq3ocQs/0008.jpg)

**Appearance.** A light-blue page places the Eye Navi script logo at the top. Two large dark-blue circular buttons sit side by side: 道案内 on the left and お散歩 on the right. Each has a light outline and white label. A smaller pale rounded 設定 button is centered below. A small version line is near the lower-left edge.

**Operation.** Choose Route guidance to select a destination, Stroll for recognition without a route, or Settings to configure the app. The demonstration explores buttons with VoiceOver and activates the chosen item. The Settings route leads to S02.

**Interpretation/limits.** The large walking choices dominate visually; configuration is smaller and lower. This frame is from a historical tutorial, not the current launch design. The right-hand text and red arrow belong to the tutorial presentation.

**English labels/translation.** 道案内 = Route guidance; お散歩 = Stroll; 設定 = Settings.

## S02 — Settings menu and startup option

![S02 full uncropped reference frame](../../capstone_research/EyeNavi_Research/evidence/QffWGq3ocQs/0030.jpg)

Visible rows are home management, destination management, announced obstacles/landmarks, nearby facilities, and guidance speech speed. A startup switch appears lower down.

**English labels/translation.** 自宅の登録編集削除 = Register/edit/delete home. 目的地の登録編集削除 = Register/edit/delete destinations. 案内する障害物 目標物 = Obstacles and landmarks to announce. 案内する周辺施設 = Nearby facilities to announce.
